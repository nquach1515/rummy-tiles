Privacy

Rummy Tiles has no accounts and stores nothing about you on a server.

  • The game runs in the host's browser. Your name and a random device id are kept in your own browser's storage so you can rejoin a table, and a game in progress is saved there so it can be resumed.
  • Online tables connect through a small relay that only passes messages between the players at a table while they're connected. It keeps no logs of games, names or messages.
  • There are no ads, analytics or trackers.
